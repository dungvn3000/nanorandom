🔍 How camera entropy works

# 1. Fresh CSPRNG salt (secret, never sent anywhere) salt = crypto.getRandomValues(32 bytes) # 2. Camera frames — sampled pixel bytes pixels = sample(N frames × 256 random pixels × RGBA) ↳ physical sensor noise + screen content (blockchain chars if aimed at Matrix Rain) # 3. Combine and hash entropy = SHA-256( salt ++ pixels )[0 : ENT_bytes] mnemonic = BIP39( entropy )

The pixel sampling positions are chosen by the CSPRNG, so even a static image cannot be replayed predictably. Camera sensor thermal noise adds physical entropy invisible to any attacker. Pointing the camera at the Nano Matrix Rain adds a third layer: live blockchain hashes encoded as light.

Security note: Camera access is processed entirely in your browser via getUserMedia + canvas.getImageData(). No frames are ever transmitted to any server. Generated seeds are displayed in plaintext — use in a private location.
