How it works. Clicking Generate Seed combines two inputs via SHA-256: a fresh 32-byte CSPRNG salt (crypto.getRandomValues) and camera pixel samples — 256 CSPRNG-chosen random pixel positions sampled across 32 frames (768–24 576 bytes of visual data). The pixel positions themselves are chosen by the CSPRNG so an attacker cannot predict which pixels were read. Output is truncated to the BIP39 entropy length (16–32 bytes) and a standard BIP39 checksum is appended.

Point at the Nano Matrix Rain to add a third layer: live Nano blockchain blockhashes encoded as visible light on your screen, re-captured as photons by the camera sensor. An attacker must know your CSPRNG state and the exact block window and the camera sensor noise — three independent secrets simultaneously.
